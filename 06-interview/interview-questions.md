## 2. Interview Questions

### A. Background
1. How long have you worked in software testing?
2. What is your current role in the QA team?

### B. Testing Process
3. How do you usually plan software testing?
4. How do you determine test scenarios and test cases?
5. How do you choose the testing method?

### C. Defect Management
6. How do you report and classify bugs?
7. What is the difference between severity and priority
   in your team's workflow?
8. How do you perform retesting?

### D. Tools and Documentation
9. What tools do you use for testing?
10. How do you document testing activities?

### E. Standards and Professional Advice
11. Does your team refer to ISO or IEEE standards?
12. What advice would you give to students
    conducting their first QA project?
